## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

At **Synthetix Global Inc.** ("the Company"), our mission is to build intelligent, resilient, and ethical enterprise software solutions. This **Code of Conduct & Workplace Ethics** establishes the standard of behavior expected from every team member. We are committed to fostering an inclusive, safe, and transparent working environment rooted in integrity, mutual respect, and accountability.

## 3. Equal Opportunity & Anti-Harassment Policy

### 3.1 Non-Discrimination Statement
Synthetix Global is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We strictly prohibit discrimination or harassment based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, or national origin
- Gender identity, gender expression, or sexual orientation
- Religion, creed, or spiritual beliefs
- Age, physical or mental disability, or genetic information
- Marital status, pregnancy, or veteran status

### 3.2 Anti-Harassment & Anti-Bullying Guidelines
Harassment of any kind—whether verbal, physical, visual, or digital—will not be tolerated. Examples of prohibited conduct include:
1. Unwelcome romantic advances, physical touching, or suggestive remarks.
2. Derogatory jokes, slurs, memes, or offensive commentary shared via email, Slack, or any internal channels.
3. Microaggressions, intimidation, public humiliation, or persistent deliberate exclusion from team activities.

---

## 4. Professional Conduct & Conflict of Interest

### 4.1 Outside Employment & Board Memberships
Employees must avoid outside activities, financial investments, or secondary employment ("moonlighting") that conflict with the Company's business interests or impair job performance.
- Any secondary employment or advisory role must be pre-approved in writing by the **Chief Legal Officer (CLO)**.
- Working for or consulting with a direct competitor or vendor is strictly prohibited.

### 4.2 Gifts, Entertainment, and Bribery (FCPA Compliance)
We strictly adhere to international anti-bribery laws, including the **U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA)** and the **UK Bribery Act**.
- **Threshold Limit:** Employees may not accept or offer gifts or entertainment valued in excess of **$100 USD** without prior approval from the Compliance Office.
- **Government Officials:** Providing gifts, meals, or favors to government officials or regulatory agents is strictly forbidden.

## 5. Whistleblower Protection & Reporting Mechanisms

Employees who observe violations of this Code, company policies, or the law have a duty to report them promptly.

### Reporting Channels:
- **Direct Manager or People Partner:** For initial escalation and guidance.
- **Ethics Hotline (24/7, Anonymous):** `+1-800-555-ETHX` (Access Code: `SYNTH-SECURE`)
- **Web Portal:** `https://ethics-portal.synthetixglobal.internal`
- **Direct Email:** `compliance@synthetixglobal.com`

> **Non-Retaliation Guarantee:** Synthetix Global strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports a suspected violation in good faith or participates in an investigation.

## 6. Disciplinary Matrix

Violations of this policy will result in formal disciplinary action, assessed on a case-by-case basis depending on severity:

```
[ Minor Infraction ] ──> Informal Coaching & Written Warning
          │
[ Moderate Breach ]  ──> Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) & Suspension
          │
[ Severe Misconduct] ──> Immediate Termination of Employment & Potential Legal Action
```
