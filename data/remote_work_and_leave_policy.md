# Synthetix Global Inc. — Remote Work, Leaves, & Expense Policy

**Document ID:** POL-OPS-2026-009  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Owner:** People Operations & Global Finance  

---

## 1. Overview & Eligibility

Synthetix Global operates as a **Remote-First** organization. This policy sets expectations for hybrid and remote employees regarding working hours, digital etiquette, paid time off, and operational expense reimbursements.

---

## 2. Remote Work Guidelines

### 2.1 Workspace Requirements
- Employees must maintain a dedicated, ergonomic workspace equipped with high-speed internet (minimum **50 Mbps download / 15 Mbps upload**).
- Remote employees must ensure confidential conversations cannot be overheard in shared spaces (use of noise-canceling headsets required).

### 2.2 Core Collaborative Hours
To balance flexibility with cross-functional collaboration, all employees are expected to be available for synchronous communication during their designated regional core window:

- **Americas (EST/PST):** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM EST
- **EMEA (GMT/CET):** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM CET
- **APAC (SGT/JST):** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM SGT

---

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Allocations

Synthetix Global offers a comprehensive leave structure designed to promote well-being, rest, and personal balance.

### 3.1 Annual Leave Summary

| Leave Category | Annual Allowance | Accrual / Carryover Rules | Notice Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flexible PTO** | 25 Days / Year | Accrues monthly (2.08 days/mo); max 5 days carryover into Q1. | 2 weeks advance notice for >3 consecutive days. |
| **Sick & Mental Health** | 12 Days / Year | Front-loaded on Jan 1; does not carry over. | Same-day notification via Slack `#out-of-office`. |
| **Parental Leave** | 16 Weeks (100% Paid) | Available to primary/secondary caregivers (birth, adoption, foster). | 30 days written notice. |
| **Bereavement Leave** | Up to 10 Days | Fully paid; applies to immediate family members. | As soon as reasonably practical. |
| **Civic & Jury Duty** | Up to 5 Days | Fully paid with official court summons documentation. | 5 business days advance notice. |

### 3.2 Sabbatical Program
Employees with **5 or more years of continuous full-time service** are eligible to take a **4-week paid sabbatical** to pursue personal growth, travel, or volunteer endeavors.

---

## 4. Home Office Stipends & Expense Reimbursements

### 4.1 Equipment Setup Allowances
- **One-Time Initial Setup Stipend:** Up to **$1,500 USD** reimbursed upon onboarding for desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, docking station, and lighting.
- **Annual Technology Refresh:** Up to **$500 USD/year** for peripherals, cables, keyboards, and ergonomic accessories.
- **Monthly Connectivity Allowance:** **$75 USD/month** credited automatically to payroll for home high-speed internet / mobile data.

### 4.2 Business Travel & Per Diem Rates
When traveling on company business, employees must adhere to the following travel guidelines:
- **Airfare:** Economy class for flights under 6 hours; Premium Economy or Business Class allowed for international flights exceeding 6 continuous hours (VP approval required).
- **Lodging:** Maximum **$250 USD/night** (standard cities) or **$350 USD/night** (high-cost metropolitan tiers: NYC, London, SF, Tokyo).
- **Daily Meals & Incidentals (Per Diem):** Up to **$85 USD/day** (itemized receipts required via Expensify within 30 days).

---

## 5. Equipment Return & Offboarding

Upon separation of employment:
1. All company-owned equipment (laptop, monitor, security tokens) must be returned via prepaid shipping label provided by IT within **7 business days**.
2. Personal access tokens, cloud service authentications, and cached data must be permanently erased.
